## Do I need an account?

| Action | Account needed? |
| --- | --- |
| Chat or send files to a paired device | No |
| Join a room by invitation | No |
| Create a room | Yes |
| Find your signed-in devices and sync settings | Yes |

Choose **Login** in the header to sign up or sign in. It becomes **Account** after sign-in. There is no password recovery flow.

## Connect your signed-in devices

Online devices signed into the same account discover and connect to each other privately. They appear in Devices marked **This is yours**; choose one to open a chat. Signing in does not open a chat by itself.

Each browser profile keeps its own device identity. Signing in does not merge identities or share device keys. Concurrent tabs in one profile share an identity; the newest tab takes the connection.

Account-only connections require both devices to be online. For offline delivery, pair devices by code or QR first. Blocking and verified-only settings still apply.

## Settings sync

While signed in, settings sync automatically. A change on one device is saved to your account right away, and the most recent save applies everywhere. Other devices pick it up the next time they load the app or open **Settings** or **Account**, without a reload.

This syncs theme, incoming-file policy, verified-only mode, the relay preference, and whether your new rooms need approval. A new account starts from the settings of the device that created it; signing in elsewhere replaces that device's settings with the account's.

Messages, files, private keys, pairings, trust records, and advertising consent are not synced.

## Sign out or delete an account

**Sign out** clears that browser's chats, received files, and drafts. It disconnects the browser from its peers and rooms. Signing back in does not restore account-chat history from other devices.

To delete an account, open **Account → Delete account** and confirm your password. This removes saved preferences and signs out connected devices.

Files saved outside Evakage and other people's copies remain.

## For server operators

Docker enables accounts by default and keeps the database in the `account-data` volume. Accounts and saved settings survive container replacement; login sessions do not.

For local Node development, set `ACCOUNTS_DB=./data/accounts.sqlite`. Set `ACCOUNTS_DB=` to disable accounts.

The database contains usernames, salted password hashes, and selected settings. It contains no chat, files, or device private keys.

Behind an HTTPS reverse proxy, set `TRUST_PROXY=1` and preserve the public host header. See [configuration](/hosting/configuration#https-reverse-proxies) and [backups](/hosting/deployment#updates-and-backups).
