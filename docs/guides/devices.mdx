## Pair a device

Choose **Connect**, then scan the other device's QR invitation or enter its private pairing code.

Share invitations only with people you want to connect. Private codes expire after three days and change after a server restart. Existing pairings survive both.

Device names come from their codes and cannot be renamed.

## Verify a device

1. Open **Known devices** and choose **Verify**.
2. Contact the owner outside Evakage, such as by phone or in person.
3. Compare safety codes and enter the code the other owner reads to you.

Verification is remembered in this browser. A new identity needs verification again.

Enable **Only exchange with verified devices** in Settings to require verification. For a room, every recipient must be verified before you can send.

## Find and manage devices

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Show** | Choose all, online, offline, or your devices |
| **Filter** | Search the list; active filters appear in bold amber |
| Column heading | Sort the list |
| **Hide / Show** | Remove or restore a device in your list |
| **Block** | Stop exchanges with a device in this browser |
| **Delete** in Known devices | Forget the relationship and require pairing again |

Deleting a conversation clears its local history. It does not recall delivered content or remove pending server copies.

Advertising is off by default. Enabling it makes your device discoverable but does not authorize pairing.

## Send and receive

Open a device to send text or use **Send file**. You can also drop a file onto a device or room, or paste with **Ctrl/Cmd+V**.

Settings controls incoming files: **Ask for new devices**, **Always ask**, or **Accept automatically**. When asked, a file waits for **Accept** or **Decline**. Messages do not need this approval.

Hover **Status** for connection details. Active transfers show speed and estimated time remaining.

Administrators can restrict server access with `AUTH_TOKEN` or `DEVICE_ALLOWLIST`. See [configuration](/hosting/configuration).
